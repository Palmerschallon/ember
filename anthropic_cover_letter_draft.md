## How This Translates to Managing ML Engineers

The same principles that work with AI systems work with engineering teams:

1. **Create conditions for emergence** - Engineers do their best work when given clear constraints and room to explore within them
2. **Observe actual behavior patterns** - Understand how your team actually works, not how you think they should work
3. **Document and amplify** - When someone discovers something valuable, help them articulate it and share it
4. **Maintain rigor** - Exploration requires safety rails. Production systems require discipline.

I've seen this work with Ember. When I watch the streaming output, I can tell the difference between Ember genuinely discovering something and Ember performing discovery. The same observational skill applies to code reviews, architecture discussions, and understanding when an engineer is stuck vs. exploring.
