## TROUBLESHOOTING

**Can't connect?**
1. Check both Quest 3 and PC are on same WiFi
2. Verify IP: `ip addr show | grep inet`
3. Check backend is running: `pgrep -f ember_cloud.py`

**Security warning won't go away?**
- This is normal for self-signed certs
- Safe to proceed (it's your local network)
- Or enable Developer Mode on Quest for easier testing
