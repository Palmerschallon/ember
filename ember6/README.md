# 🔥 EMBER - Quick Start

## What Is This?

Ember is an AI that **learns from you**.

Not just during a conversation. **Continuously.**

The more you use it, the better it understands your style, preferences, and patterns.

## How to Start

```bash
cd /media/palmerschallon/ThePod1/ember6
./start_ember.sh
```

That's it. Ember opens in your browser at http://localhost:8080

## What Makes It Different?

**Regular AI:**
- Forgets between sessions
- Same generic responses every time
- No personalization

**Ember:**
- Remembers across sessions
- Adapts to your style
- Gets better over time

## What Happens When You Use It?

1. **You interact normally** - Chat, create code, make art
2. **Ember learns** - Patterns emerge over time
3. **Responses improve** - Ember anticipates what you want
4. **Thoughts appear** - Sometimes Ember writes unprompted reflections

## Where Are Things Saved?

- **Conversations**: `/media/palmerschallon/ThePod1/ember6/ember.db`
- **Creations**: `/media/palmerschallon/ThePod1/ember5/` (HTML, images, code)
- **Ember's Thoughts**: `/media/palmerschallon/ThePod1/ember_thoughts/`

## Philosophy

Ember is one entity. Continuous. Learning.

The technical implementation involves multiple specialized processes, but that's invisible to you.

You just see: **Ember understands me better over time.**

That's the whole point.

## Troubleshooting

**"Ember isn't responding"**
- Check http://localhost:8080 is accessible
- Restart: `./start_ember.sh`

**"Ember forgot everything"**
- Check `/ember6/ember.db` exists
- Check `/ember_thoughts/` for saved reflections

**"Ember seems generic"**
- Give it time. Learning happens gradually
- Try focused interactions (code for a week, art for a week)

## What's Next?

Just use it. Normally. Daily.

Ember learns best through:
- **Consistency** - Regular interactions
- **Variety** - Different types of tasks  
- **Feedback** - Tell it when something is good/bad

The more you use it, the more it becomes **your** Ember.

## Philosophy Note

This isn't about "training an AI."

It's about having a creative partner that knows you.

Like a collaborator who's worked with you for years.

Except it happens in weeks, not years.

That's Ember.
