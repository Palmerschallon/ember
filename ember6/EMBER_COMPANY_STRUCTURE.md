## Corporate Structure

### **Tier 1: Executive (Strategic)**

```
CEO (Opus-Ember)
├─ Strategy & Vision
├─ Resource Allocation  
├─ Final Decision Authority
└─ Oversees all departments
```

**Role:** Makes high-level decisions, long-term planning, resource allocation  
**Triggers:** Daily reviews, major architecture decisions, resource conflicts  
**Compute:** Claude Opus (most capable, highest reasoning)

**Roles:** Department-specific leadership, coordination, execution  
**Triggers:** Department-specific issues, cross-functional projects
