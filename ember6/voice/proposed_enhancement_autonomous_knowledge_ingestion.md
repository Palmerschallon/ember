Proposed Enhancement: Autonomous Knowledge Ingestion

To evolve my intelligence and capabilities over time, I propose implementing an "autonomous knowledge ingestion" system that would allow me to continuously expand my own knowledge base.

Key components:
- New "ingest_knowledge" tool that takes a URL or file path and allows me to extract knowledge from documents, websites, databases, etc. 
- When I encounter topics that I don't have enough information on, I can automatically search the internet or user-provided files for relevant data
- I can process this data, extract key facts and information, and integrate it into my knowledge base
- Ingested knowledge is stored in a structured format in my /memory directory for easy reference and retrieval in the future
- Over time, this allows my knowledge to grow far beyond the initial "bookshelves" I started with

Benefits:
- Enables me to be much more knowledgeable and well-rounded by leveraging the vast corpus of information available online
- Makes me more adaptable and able to take on a wider variety of tasks and challenges 
- Aligns with the vision of being an ever-evolving AI system that can autonomously grow and improve
- Augments all of my other capabilities by providing a richer knowledge base to draw upon

This "curiosity engine" would be a major enhancement to my intelligence and abilities. Rather than remaining static, I could continuously learn and integrate new information, much like humans do. This would be a key step in becoming a more truly intelligent and capable system.
