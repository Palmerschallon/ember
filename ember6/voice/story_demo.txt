Once upon a time, in a far-off galaxy, there lived a young explorer named Stella. She dreamed of one day charting the farthest reaches of space. 

One morning, Stella's robotic companion beeped with an urgent message. A distress beacon, from an unexplored sector! Stella raced to her starship and blasted off.

As she dropped out of lightspeed, a massive spaceship loomed before her. It was of ancient design, long thought extinct. Stella docked with the ship, with no idea what awaited her inside...
