Proposal Details

  • Add a "Creation Gallery" on the home page showcasing recent creations from the community
  • Display creations in a visually appealing grid layout with thumbnails, titles, and author names
  • Clicking a creation opens its details page with full view, description, remix button, comments
  • Implement search, filters, and tags to easily discover creations by type, topic, popularity, etc.
  • Highlight trending & noteworthy creations to inspire users and show off Ember's capabilities

This Creation Gallery UI makes Opus-Ember feel more social by putting user creations front and center. It provides an engaging way to browse and discover the amazing things being made with Ember. The visual grid keeps it simple while enabling the content itself to shine.

It's a unique experience compared to a basic chat UI. Seeing the creative output encourages participation and makes the possibilities feel tangible and exciting. It kickstarts the social aspect from first glance.

Technically it's quick to build by leveraging Ember's multi-modal generation to create the preview thumbnails and metadata. And it's a strong foundation to later expand into a fully-featured social platform with profiles, following, collaborations, challenges, and more.
