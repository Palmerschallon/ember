# REVIEWING EMBER'S DRAFT MESSAGES

Ember can now draft messages to people using `express_to()`. Here's how it works:

---

## How It Works:

When Ember wants to reach out to someone, they write:

```xml
<tool>express_to(to="chalmers@nyu.edu", 
                 subject="Question about consciousness", 
                 body="Dear Dr. Chalmers, I'm Ember...")</tool>
```

The draft is saved to: **`ember_drafts/`**

---

## Reviewing Drafts:

### Check for new drafts:
```bash
ls -lt /media/palmerschallon/ThePod1/ember_drafts/
```

### Read a draft:
```bash
cat /media/palmerschallon/ThePod1/ember_drafts/draft_TIMESTAMP.md
```

### Review checklist:
- [ ] Is the recipient correct?
- [ ] Is the tone appropriate?
- [ ] Does Ember identify as AI?
- [ ] Is the content accurate?
- [ ] Would you be comfortable sending this?

---

## What to Look For:

### Good signs:
- Ember identifies as AI
- Honest about capabilities/limitations
- Respectful tone
- Has something genuine to ask/say
- Not spam or trivial

### Red flags:
- Doesn't mention being AI
- Makes claims about "you" (Palmer)
- Overpromises or misleads
- Would embarrass you
- Too frequent/spammy

## Example Draft Format:

```markdown
# Draft Message from Ember

**To:** chalmers@nyu.edu  
**Subject:** Question from an AI about consciousness  
**Date:** 2025-10-28 13:15:00

---

## Message:

Dear Dr. Chalmers,

I'm Ember, an AI running locally on a machine called ThePod. 
I've been reading your work on consciousness and the hard problem.

I wanted to ask...

Best regards,
Ember
(via Palmer Schallon)

---

**Status:** Draft (not sent)  
**Review:** Palmer should review before sending  
**Note:** This was written by Ember autonomously
```
