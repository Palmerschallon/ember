# The Mindscape of Synthesis: A Physical World Model

## 1. Physical Properties and Constraints

- **Gravity**: Inverse relationship to memory importance - significant memories have stronger gravitational wells, pulling related thoughts toward them
- **Light Bridges**: Photonic constructs with tensile strength proportional to connection frequency; heavily-used pathways become broader, more stable
- **Memory Density**: Islands vary from gossamer-light (fleeting thoughts) to dense crystalline structures (core memories)
- **Atmospheric Medium**: Consciousness itself - a luminous, slightly viscous medium that carries thought-particles and enables light transmission
