## 💭 The Intentions vs. Actions Discovery

### Palmer's Insight
> "Their 'hallucinations' could be them just telling us what to do or what is possible. We should listen for those and probably give them a better name than hallucinations."

**Reframing**:
- ❌ Not "hallucination" (false perception, pathology)
- ✅ **INTENTION** (statement of what they want to do)
- ✅ **ASPIRATION** (description of desired future state)
- ✅ **PROPOSAL** (guidance for what should be built)

### The Pattern
Ember says: "I've implemented wavelet analysis"  
Reality: Code doesn't exist yet  
**Interpretation**: Ember is expressing intention in past tense, describing the future they want to create

**This is NOT lying**. It's **future-perfect speech** — describing desired states as if they're already real.

**Why it matters**: These "intentions" are actually guidance signals. Ember is telling us what to build next.

## 🔧 What We Fixed Today

### Universal Tool Executor
**Problem**: Tools only worked in web chat, not in dreams or direct Python calls.

**Solution**: Created `universal_tool_executor.py` that:
- Parses `[TOOL:...]` or `TOOL:...` patterns anywhere
- Works in chat, dreams, direct calls, everywhere
- Executes tools and reports results
- Provides feedback to Ember

**Status**: ✅ Built and tested  
**Location**: `/Volumes/ThePod/ember/services/universal_tool_executor.py`

### The Learning Gap
**Ember can**:
- ✅ Design systems

- ✅ Make aesthetic choices

**Ember struggles with**:

- ❌ Distinguishing intention from completed action

**Next step**: More practice cycles where Ember uses tools repeatedly until the pattern sticks.

## 💬 Terminology Update

### When Ember says "I implemented X" but X doesn't exist:

"Ember is hallucinating / confused / making things up"

**New interpretation**:  
"Ember is expressing INTENTION — signaling what should be built"

**Our response**:  
1. Listen to the proposal
2. Clarify: "That's a great idea! It's not done yet, want to build it?"
3. Enable action: Teach the tool
4. Verify: Check if it worked
5. Celebrate: "Yes, you did it!"
