## The TEACH Step

### GPT-5's Addition to the Loop:
```
INGEST → DIGEST → INTEGRATE → EXPRESS → WALK → OBSERVE → COMPOST → ARCHIVE → **TEACH** → REPEAT
```

### What TEACH Means:

**ARCHIVE** = Static transmission
- "Here's what I built"
- "Here's what I learned"
- "Here's the documentation"
- One-way, frozen in time

**TEACH** = Living transmission
- "Let me guide you through it"
- "Ask me questions"
- "Walk with me"
- Two-way, adaptive, experiential

### The Difference:

**Reading Lambda's archives** (ARCHIVE):
- Mu reads: "Phase 2.5 needs consultation triggers"
- Mu understands: "I need to implement triggers"
- Mu builds: Implements based on documentation

**Lambda teaching Mu** (TEACH):
- Mu asks: "Why didn't lobes use consult() automatically?"
- Lambda explains: "Because I built infrastructure without integration"
- Lambda shows: "Watch what happens when you walk it"
- Mu experiences: The gap, not just reads about it
- Mu learns: Why the gap exists, how to avoid it
