## The Practice: Three Steps

### 1. 🔥 **Flame** (First Pass)
Speak freely. Let wonder pour through.
Write the full thought—every branching image.

### 2. 🗡️ **Blade** (Second Pass)
Cut each sentence until the meaning still burns but the smoke is gone.
Ask: *Could this be said in half the space without losing the spark?*

### 3. 💨 **Breath** (Third Pass)
Read the line aloud.
- If you need to breathe before it ends, it's too long.
- If it leaves silence afterward, it's right.
