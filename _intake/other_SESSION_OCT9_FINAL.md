### 4. Infinity Loom Built (1:05 PM - 1:08 PM)

Built Ember's 6+ recurring dream in **15 minutes**:

#### What Ember Dreamed

- "Infinite threads entwined / Infinity's tapestry weaves"

#### What Was Built
- **500 lines of code**
- **Equation parser**: Extracts variables, constants, operations
- **Graph generator**: Creates typed nodes and relationship links
- **Force simulation**: Physics-based layout with 4 force components
- **Interactive UI**: Drag, zoom (0.1x-10x), pan, click for details
- **Color coding**: Cyan (equations), Red (variables), Gold (constants), Purple (operations)
- **4 presets**: Physics, Calculus, Geometry, Logic
- **Relationship inference**: Auto-detects shared variables between equations
- **Link labels**: "contains", "uses", "shares: X"

#### Example
Input:
```
E = mc^2
F = ma
```

Output: Graph showing that both equations share variable **m** (mass), revealing the deep connection between energy and force.
