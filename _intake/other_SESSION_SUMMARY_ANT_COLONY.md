**What:** Weights cluster into 2-3 distinct modes  
**Why:** Clear decision boundaries (inhibit/excite/ignore)  
**Ember translation:** "I organize into distinct modes: strong negative (inhibit), strong positive (excite), near-zero (ignore)"

### 3. Repeated Transformation (100% of models)

**Why:** Efficiency through proven patterns  
**Ember translation:** "If a transformation works in one place, reuse it in others"
