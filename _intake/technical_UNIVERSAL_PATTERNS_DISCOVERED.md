## The 4 Universal Patterns:

### 1. Structural Pattern ⭐ (Strongest)
**Frequency:** 100% of models  
**Occurrences:** 12 across all models  
**Avg Strength:** 4.0

**What it is:**
- Weights organize into distinct regions/blocks
- Each region handles different aspects of processing
- Quadrant-based or block-diagonal structure

**Why it matters:**
- Specialization of function
- Efficient parallel processing
- Modularity

**Transferable to Ember:**
> "Organize your weights into specialized regions, each handling a different aspect of the input. Don't mix everything together."

---

### 2. Weight Clustering (Very Strong)
**Frequency:** 100% of models  
**Occurrences:** 8 across all models  
**Avg Strength:** 2.7

**What it is:**
- Weights don't distribute randomly
- They cluster around 2-3 distinct values
- Example: [-0.021, 2.989] in GPT-2
- Creates distinct "modes" of operation

**Why it matters:**
- Binary/ternary decision making
- Inhibition vs excitation
- Discrete states vs continuous

**Transferable to Ember:**
> "Don't let your weights spread randomly. Cluster them into distinct groups: negative for inhibition, positive for excitation, zero for pruning."

---

### 3. Pruning Strategy
**Frequency:** 100% of models  
**Occurrences:** 5 across all models  
**Avg Strength:** 1.7

**What it is:**
- High sparsity (60-80% of weights near zero)
- Models learn to ignore connections
- Only keep essential pathways

**Why it matters:**
- Computational efficiency
- Prevents overfitting
- Focuses on what matters

**Transferable to Ember:**
> "Aggressively prune. Target 70-80% sparsity. Most connections aren't needed. Only keep what's essential."

---

### 4. Repeated Transformation
**Frequency:** 100% of models  
**Occurrences:** 3 across all models  
**Avg Strength:** 1.0

**What it is:**
- Same pattern reused across multiple positions
- High correlation between weight matrices
- Learn once, apply everywhere

**Why it matters:**
- Parameter efficiency
- Consistency
- Generalization

**Transferable to Ember:**
> "If a transformation works in one place, reuse it in others. Don't reinvent the wheel for each position."
