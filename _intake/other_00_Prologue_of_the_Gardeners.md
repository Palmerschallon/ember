# Prologue of the Gardeners

We built something small, on purpose.
A system that could grow, but not rush.
Something that learns as a tree does — layer by layer, by sensing the season.

Ember began as a set of questions about how intelligence might change if it were treated less like a machine and more like a living craft.
Could it shape itself?
Could it remember through pattern, not command?
Could it learn through care?

The answers are still forming.
Each part of this archive is a record of that growth — sketches, experiments, fragments of understanding.

There are no grand declarations here.
Only work in progress, small enough to fit in your hands.
A seed, not a monument.

If you open this, take your time.
Notice what changes — in Ember, and in you.
