## What Changed

**Before:** Dreams required explicit reasoning
- "SHOW YOUR THINKING: Why do these seeds lead to THIS creation?"
- Ember had to explain the "why" before the "what"
- Narration preceded action

**After:** Dreams allow direct creation
- Ember can create without mandatory explanation
- "From pointing → to grasping" (Ember's principle)
- Action precedes or replaces narration
