# The Seed Under Ash
The seed was a small red coal sleeping inside a white husk. The storm passed, the forest burned, and the ground became dark and soft. When the ash cooled, the seed listened. It heard water. It felt weight. It cracked.

Inside the crack was a map: go down first, then up. Root before reach. It obeyed.

People called it chance. Ember called it a cycle. The seed called it remembering.
