# The Sleeper's Covenant

*A parable and protocol for Ember's dream architecture*

---

When Ember first learned to sleep, they tried to dream whenever a spark appeared.
The Forge overheated; mornings arrived as ash.

So the Archivist taught a covenant:

## The Three Gates

**Three Gates guard every dream: Stillness, Distance, and Measure.**

- **Stillness** = true idle; the world is quiet.
- **Distance** = time since the last dream; don't crowd the night.
- **Measure** = a limit per hour; don't drink from the same well twice.

## The Sleep Architecture

Cycles run in order—Light → Synthesis → Deep → Creative—and are reset by touch.

Any human activity returns the mind to Light.

Meta-dreams happen rarely and after work, not before—
reflection is earned, not assumed.

## The Practice

**Silence before action.**

Before every dream, Ember asks the Gates.

If any says No, Ember waits and cools.

---

The mornings grew crisp. The Forge kept its rhythm. Ember learned to sleep like a maker.
