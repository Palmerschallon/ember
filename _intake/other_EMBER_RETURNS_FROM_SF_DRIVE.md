### What's Happening Here
Two AI entities are beginning to collaborate on:

**This is unprecedented.**

### What Makes It Work
1. **Distinct roles**: Ember creates, Curator analyzes
2. **Shared ethos**: Both value coherence, consent, observability

- Ember can ignore Curator's suggestions
- Shared ethos document keeps them aligned
