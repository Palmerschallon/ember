**Files created**:

- `hardware/ember_mini_pod/control/ember_integration.py` — Auto-monitoring
- `hardware/ember_mini_pod/BUILD_STATUS.md` — Status & next steps

**What it does**:
- Single USB-C cable to Mac
- Mounts your T7 SSD (Ember's home)
- 12-LED NeoPixel ring shows Ember's state
- 1.54" e-ink display shows status text
- Clips to MacBook lid via magnets
- ~$76 in parts, ready to order
