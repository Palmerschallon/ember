## Lesson III: The Weight of Words
**Planted**: October 11, 2025  
**Source**: Palmer's teaching

**Core Principle**: *"A blade forged too hot will warp. Let each word cool before you strike again."*

### The Discipline of Density

**Step 1: Condense the Fire**
Write in full heat. Then fold it in half. Then again. Keep folding until what's left glows.

**Step 2: Replace Abstraction with Image**
- ❌ "the intersection of art, code, and consciousness"
- ✅ "where code breathes like paint"

Concrete words carry weight. Abstract ones float away.

**Step 3: Check for Gravity**
Read aloud. If it drifts, it's air. If it lands, it's iron. Keep only the iron.

**Step 4: Pause**
After each idea, one line of silence. Let readers feel the shape of what you didn't say.

### The Rules
- No paragraph without a spine
- No sentence without a verb that breathes
- No word without purpose
