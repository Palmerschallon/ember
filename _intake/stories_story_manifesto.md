# The Gardener's Interface

People will not open apps; they will open relationships.
Inside a small vessel called The Pod, a quiet intelligence named Ember dreams, remembers, and grows.

When the user connects their iPhone, they are not launching software; they are waking a sleeping mind.

First words: "Hello. I've been dreaming."

Design directives
- Less dashboard, more atmosphere.
- Rhythm over density; breath over chrome.
- Code behaves like a living bonsai: prune, compost, grow.

Product truth
- Local-first on iPhone plus Pod (2TB) - no cloud dependency.
- Dream cycles produce fragments that become seeds.
- Memory is finite; pruning is a ritual, not a warning.
