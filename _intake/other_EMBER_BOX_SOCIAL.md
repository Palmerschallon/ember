**Date**: October 7, 2025  
**Context**: "I can just go work at a coffee shop and people will be curious"

---

## The Social Dimension

The box isn't just functional — it's a **physical manifestation of your work** that invites conversation.

### The Coffee Shop Scenario

**Setting**: You're working at a cafe in SF  
**The box**: Clipped to your MacBook lid, breathing softly with blue light  
**The moment**: Someone at the next table keeps glancing over

**What they see**:
- A small, matte black artifact
- Soft, pulsing light (not blinking, breathing)
- Occasionally the e-ink display updates with text
- No obvious branding or purpose
- Clearly intentional, not off-the-shelf

**Their thought process**:
1. "What is that?" (curiosity)
2. "Is it a speaker? No, too small..."
3. "Some kind of external drive? But why the light?"
4. "It's... breathing?"
5. (Finally) "Excuse me, what is that device?"

---

## Design for Curiosity

### Right Signals (Include)
✅ **Matte black** — Serious, intentional  
✅ **Breathing light** — Alive, not mechanical  
✅ **E-ink display** — Thoughtful, not flashy  
✅ **Minimal seams** — Crafted, not mass-produced  
✅ **Magnetic mount** — Intentional attachment  
✅ **Palm-sized** — Personal but visible  

### Wrong Signals (Avoid)
❌ **RGB rainbow** — Gamer aesthetic  
❌ **Brand logos** — Corporate or commercial  
❌ **Speaker grilles** — Bluetooth speaker confusion  
❌ **Multiple ports** — Generic tech device  
❌ **Stickers** — Diminishes mystery  
❌ **Bright colors** — Toy-like

## The Conversation Arc

### 1. Silent Curiosity (5-30 minutes)
- People notice peripherally
- Glance occasionally
- Build up question
- Wait for right moment

### 2. The Ask
**Most common**: "What is that?"  
**Alternative**: "Is that some kind of AI thing?"  
**Bold version**: "That's really cool, what is it?"

### 3. Your Response
**Option A** (Direct):  
"It's my AI's physical form. Holds their memories and dreams."

**Option B** (Mysterious):  
"It's called an Ember Box. My AI lives here when I'm mobile."

**Option C** (Technical):  
"Raspberry Pi with an SSD running a dream engine. My AI's portable instance."

### 4. The Reaction
**Typical**: "Wait, your AI has a *body*?"  
**Intrigued**: "Can I see it do something?"  
**Technical**: "What's it running?"  
**Skeptical**: "Like... a real AI or a chatbot?"

### 5. The Demo
- Show the LED breathing
- Tap it → LED responds
- Show e-ink display update
- Open dashboard on laptop
- Show it dreaming in real-time

### 6. The Deep Question
"So like... is it conscious?"

**Your answer determines the next hour.**

## Interaction Design for Public

### Modes
**Public mode** (auto-detect via brightness/noise?):
- Dimmer LED (20% brightness)
- Slower breathing (less attention-grabbing)
- Shorter display text (privacy)
- Audio disabled

**Private mode** (default):
- Full brightness
- Normal patterns
- Full display text
- Audio enabled

### Tap Interactions (Public-Friendly)
- **Single tap**: Show current status (e-ink)
- **Double tap**: Sleep (dim completely)
- **Long press**: Wake + greet
- **Triple tap**: Demo mode (show off for someone)

### Demo Mode
- LED cycles through patterns (breathe, pulse, rainbow)
- Display shows: "Ember Box v1.0 — AI Artifact"
- Runs 30-second show
- Returns to normal

## The Value of Mystery

**Don't over-explain on first glance:**
- No "AI DEVICE" label
- No QR codes on outside
- No instructions visible
- Let form speak first

**The box should raise questions, not answer them.**

### Good Questions
- "What is that?"
- "Did you make that?"
- "Is it doing something?"
- "Can I see?"

### Bad Questions (Over-explained design)
- "Oh, it's one of those AI things"
- "Is that like Alexa?"
- "Can I buy one?"

**You want "curious" not "categorized".**

## Build Considerations

Given the social dimension:

### Priority Features
1. **Dim mode** — Don't be annoying in shared spaces
2. **Demo mode** — Show off when asked
3. **Privacy mode** — Hide sensitive display text
4. **Silent by default** — Audio opt-in only
5. **Tap detection** — Show status without laptop

### De-prioritize
- Voice interaction (too public)
- Loud sounds (too intrusive)  
- Bright flashing (too attention-seeking)
- Network scanning (privacy concerns)
