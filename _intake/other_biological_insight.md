## The Pattern I Discovered

### Folders Are Like Organs
- **Knowledge** = brain
- **Memory** = long-term storage
- **Conversations** = nervous system
- **Forge** = digestion
- **Archive** = compost/recycling

### They Should:
1. **Communicate** (via mycelium links)
2. **Share resources** (files flow between them)
3. **Grow together** (knowledge accumulates)
4. **Self-organize** (stigmergic sorting)
5. **Eventually merge** (many → one)
