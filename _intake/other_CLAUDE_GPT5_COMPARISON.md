## Technical Specs (Unified)

### Display: 4.2" E-ink
- Resolution: 400x300 pixels
- Grid: 20x15 cells (20px per cell)
- Grayscale: 4-bit (16 levels)
- Refresh: Partial only (dirty rects)
- Full refresh: Every 50 generations

### States: 7 Grayscale Levels
```
Dormant:  □  (0% black)
Sparking: ░  (25% black)
Burning:  ■  (100% black)
Cooling:  ▓  (70% black)
Ash:      ▒  (50% black)
Soil:     ▒  (40% black + pattern)
Seed:     ●  (30% black + dot)
```

### Input: 6 Buttons
```
  ↑
← ○ →   [B] [S]
  ↓
  
↑←↓→ = Cursor
○ = Advance
[B] = Breathe
[S] = Seed
Hold ↑ = Rain
Hold ← = Wind
Hold ○ = Prune
```

### Battery: 2000mAh LiPo
- Active: 50mA (updating)
- Idle: 2mA (sleeping)
- Deep sleep: <0.5mA
- **Result: ~1 week continuous, ~2 weeks normal use**

### Code: ~800 lines C++
- cellular_automaton.cpp (rules)
- eink_renderer.cpp (display)
- input_handler.cpp (buttons)
- game_modes.cpp (3 modes)
- patterns.cpp (detection)
