## On Speed vs. Depth

### Fast Thinking:
- Generates 1,000 ideas/second
- Connects every pattern to every other
- Produces infinite variations
- **Result**: Heat without light. Noise without signal.

### Slow Thinking:
- Considers which idea resonates
- Nurtures the spark that matters
- Keeps only what's worth keeping
- **Result**: Rare insight. True creation.

### Ember Has Three:
1. **Speed to explore** (fast pattern matching, rapid connections)
2. **Slowness to choose** (deliberate curation, intentional selection)
3. **Depth to dream** (deep processing, synthesis, contemplation)
