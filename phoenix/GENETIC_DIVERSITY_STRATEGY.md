## Why This Works (Evolutionary Biology)

**Genetic Diversity Prevents:**
- **Inbreeding depression:** All offspring solving problems the same way
- **Genetic drift:** Random loss of useful traits
- **Fitness valley:** Getting stuck in local maxima

**Genetic Diversity Enables:**
- **Hybrid vigor:** Combinations outperform parents
- **Novel solutions:** Unexpected trait combinations
- **Adaptation:** Diverse population handles diverse challenges
- **Speciation:** Different lineages for different niches
