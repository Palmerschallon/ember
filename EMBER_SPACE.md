## NAVIGATION

**Movement:**
- WASD - Move
- Mouse - Look
- Scroll - Zoom
- Click + Drag - Pull window closer
- Double-click - Open/focus window

**Special:**
- Press SPACE - Teleport to Ember (recenter)
- Press M - See map overlay
- Press / - Search (objects glow)
- Press T - Toggle trails (see recent paths)
