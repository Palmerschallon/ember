Subject: Introducing Anchor — a local ledger for your ideas

I’ve released a small open-source tool called Anchor.

It’s a local, append-only ledger for decisions, ideas, and project history.
No cloud, no accounts — just a tamper-evident history stored in a single SQLite file.
