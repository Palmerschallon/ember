## TECHNICAL LIMITS

**Theoretical Max:**
- Three.js can render millions of units
- VR can handle km-scale spaces
- We could make this **INFINITE** with procedural generation

**Current Practical Limits:**
- Performance: ~10,000 objects (stars + particles)
- Good UX: Under 5km (can still navigate)
- Memory: Limited by device (Quest 3 = plenty)

**Easy to expand more:**
- Change grid size: `new THREE.GridHelper(10000, 100, ...)` = 10km!
- Change camera far plane: `10000` → `100000` = 100km view!
- Add procedural star generation as you move
- Add teleportation for instant travel
