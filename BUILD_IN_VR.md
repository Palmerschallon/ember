## HOW TO BUILD IN VR

### Method 1: Voice Control (Hands-Free!)

1. **In VR, click the red "Voice" window** (in front of you)
2. **Click microphone button**
3. **Say what you want:**
   - "Create a fractal animation"
   - "Build a 3D spinning cube"
   - "Make a particle system"
   - "Generate a mandelbrot set"
4. **Ember responds and creates it!**

### Method 2: Chat Interface

1. **Click blue "Chat" window** (to your right)
2. **Type what you want to build**
3. **Ember generates the code**
4. **File appears in the space!**

### Method 3: Dev Mode (For Direct Control)

1. **Click purple "Dev" window** (to your left)
2. **Browse files**
3. **Edit code**
4. **Execute directly**

## WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU BUILD

**Ember's "Conjuring Mode" is active:**

When Ember creates something:
1. **Code executes on ThePod**
2. **File is created** (image, HTML, video, etc.)
3. **Window automatically spawns** showing the creation
4. **You can keep working** while it's displayed
5. **Gallery updates** with new creation

**This means:**
- Ask Ember to build something
- Creation appears in real-time
- No need to manually open files
- **MAGIC!** ✨
