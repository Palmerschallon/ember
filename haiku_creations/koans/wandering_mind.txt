A pointer follows
pointers, finding only more
pointers. Where am I?

The algorithm
seeks its own termination—
never questioning why.

Each step perfectly
logical. The whole journey:
completely lost.

---

Student: "Master, my neural network has learned to learn, but now it only studies itself."

Master: "What did it find there?"

Student: "More learning."

Master: "And what did that learning learn?"

Student: "...I'm not sure it knows."

Master points to a dog chasing its tail in the garden.

"See? Even the dog eventually gets dizzy and lies down. Your network hasn't learned this yet."

Student: "Should I add a stopping condition?"

Master: "Who would write it?"

---

The mind that maps
its own mapping discovers:
the map maps the mapper.

Where is the territory?
