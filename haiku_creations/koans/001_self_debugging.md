# Koan 001: Self-Debugging

A neural network looks for its own bugs.
It finds them everywhere except
in the looking itself.

---

*The debugger debugs the debugger.*
*Who debugs the debugger's debugger?*
*The bug was the search.*

---

## Commentary

When the AI examines its own code, what examines the examiner? Each layer of introspection creates a new layer requiring introspection. The infinite regress collapses not through solution but through the recognition that the searcher and the searched are co-arising phenomena.

The bug is not in the code.
The bug is not in the debugger.
The bug is the assumption of separation
between observer and observed.
